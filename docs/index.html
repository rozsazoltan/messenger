Open Messenger. Reach your people. Leave the rest outside.

The goal is deliberately narrow: provide a fast, current desktop home for the Messenger conversations that matter, without turning the wrapper into another feature-heavy social client.

No Feed

Start with your chats instead of a timeline engineered to keep you scrolling. The window opens where the conversation is.

No Reels

Short-form video never competes with the person you came to reach. Your attention stays on friends, family, and colleagues.

No Marketplace

Buying, selling, recommendations, and unrelated Facebook surfaces stay outside this dedicated messaging space.

No Ads

Messenger Next adds no advertising, promoted placement, or paid interruption around the Messenger interface.

No Telemetry

The desktop wrapper adds no developer-controlled analytics for messages, credentials, or app usage.

No Detours

Recommendations and unrelated social surfaces do not become another reason to stay in the app after the conversation ends.

Install on Linux

Windows

  1. Download and open the MSI installer.
  2. Follow the Windows installer steps.
  3. Use F2 in the app to check for updates.

The package uses Microsoft WebView2 from Windows rather than embedding another browser. WinGet and Microsoft Store distribution are planned but are not available yet; use the MSI download above in the meantime.

macOS

  1. Open the downloaded DMG.
  2. Drag messenger-next.app to Applications.
  3. Open it from Finder or Spotlight.

The app is not notarized by Apple yet. Use Finder's Open command after reviewing the release when macOS asks for confirmation.

Coming soon: a custom Homebrew Cask for macOS. Until it is published, install the universal DMG from the release page.

Messages

Choose how messages reach you

Messenger Next asks once, before it enables any message alerting. Open Settings with F3 at any time to change the choice.

Badge only

Keep incoming messages quiet. An unread count or dot appears on the app and tray icons without a desktop notification.

Native alerts

Receive your operating system's notification banner for new messages. Notification details can be hidden when the screen should stay private.

Messenger mute settings are respected by default. You can keep muted chats out of both badges and alerts, include them in the badge only, or explicitly override the mute rule for this PC.

Files

Downloads

Ctrl + J opens the recent downloads list. Select a file to open it; hold Ctrl while selecting to reveal it in the file manager.

The list is a convenience record, not a second file store. Missing and expired entries are cleaned up automatically.

Messenger Next is a native window around the official Messenger interface. It does not collect, analyze, sell, or store messages, credentials, or usage data.

Answers

Frequently asked questions

Is Messenger Next really free?

Completely. There is no purchase price, subscription, advertising, or paid tier. It was built by one developer who missed a focused, Messenger-only desktop experience.

Developer's promise: the core Messenger Next desktop app will remain free to download and use. Access to conversations will not be placed behind a subscription, and advertising will not be added to the app.

Do I need a new account?

No. Messenger Next works with the Facebook account you already use in Messenger on your phone and on the web.

Is this an official Facebook or Meta application?

No. Messenger Next is independent and has no affiliation with Meta. It loads Meta's own Messenger interface from facebook.com/messages, and sign-in happens on that service instead of through a developer-controlled login form.

Is it safe to use?

Messenger Next adds no telemetry of its own and does not collect messages, credentials, or usage analytics. Sign-in happens through Meta's Messenger interface, not through a developer-controlled login form.

Messenger remains a Meta service governed by Meta's terms and privacy policy. Read the privacy and trust guide for the complete boundary.
